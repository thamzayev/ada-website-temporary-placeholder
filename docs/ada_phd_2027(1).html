About the opportunity

Develop the next generation of scholars.

ADA University invites talented and motivated candidates to pursue doctoral studies at the University of Bologna, Politecnico di Milano, and Politecnico di Torino.

The initiative supports the University's long-term academic and research development. Upon completion of their studies, candidates are expected to return to Azerbaijan and contribute to ADA University's teaching, research, and academic development.

How the process works

ADA University conducts the initial pre-selection. Shortlisted candidates then apply directly to the relevant PhD programs at the partner universities.

Politecnico di Milano · PoliMi

Architecture and Design · Illustrative research directions

Communication design: Cultural identity and heritage narratives; new perspectives on design education and learning; general theory and practice.
Interior design: Human-artificial collaboration and participatory approaches; general theory and practice.
Design manufacturing: Digital fabrication and emerging technologies for experimental design.
Representation and survey: Advanced survey; freehand drawing and AI for spatial knowledge; AI and visual interpretation of landscape transformations; archives and verifiable cultural landscape atlases.
Architectural and urban design: Typological and morphological studies; adaptive reuse; sustainable design strategies; site-specific transformation.
Urban planning: Ecological and regenerative strategies; inclusive and climate-resilient approaches; collaborative and interactive design and simulation.
Landscape and ecology: Landscape design for ecological and cultural transformation; climate adaptation and resource regeneration.
Other directions: Architectural technology; mechanics of solids and structures; valuation and project appraisal; cultural landscape as tangible and intangible heritage.

Selection pathway

Three stages to admission

ADA University's PhD Support and Development Department will provide guidance and assistance throughout the process.

01

Pre-selection by ADA University

The Selection Committee reviews academic qualifications, English proficiency, research background, subject knowledge, motivation, and potential contribution. Shortlisted candidates are invited for an interview.

02

Apply to a partner university

ADA-selected candidates apply directly to the relevant PhD programs at the University of Bologna, Politecnico di Milano, or Politecnico di Torino, following each university's admission procedures.

03

Scholarship and funding

Admitted candidates may apply for support through the State Program for Studying Abroad (2022–2028) or explore funding arrangements through ADA University, subject to applicable conditions.

Before you apply

Eligibility and application documents

Eligibility criteria

  • Be a citizen of the Republic of Azerbaijan
  • Hold a relevant master's degree or be in the final year of a master's program
  • Demonstrate English proficiency: minimum IELTS 6.5 or equivalent
  • Have a strong academic record and demonstrated research potential
  • Present a well-defined research proposal aligned with the selected university's research expertise, preferably at pre-selection
  • Commit to returning to Azerbaijan after doctoral studies and contributing to ADA University's academic, teaching, and research activities

Teaching experience and prior research involvement will be considered an advantage.

Required documents

  1. Curriculum Vitae (CV)
  2. Statement of Purpose (maximum 500 words)
    Research interests, motivation for pursuing a PhD, and relevance of academic and professional background to the selected program.
  3. Academic Writing Sample in English
    For example, a bachelor's or master's thesis or a research paper demonstrating academic writing and research skills.
  4. Proof of English Language Proficiency
  5. Academic Transcripts (unofficial/scanned copy is acceptable)
3–4 years

PhD studies generally require three to four years of full-time study and research, depending on the host university. Candidates are expected to complete required coursework, research activities, and a doctoral dissertation.
